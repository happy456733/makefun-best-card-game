EMBER DUEL

An original duel of cards and cunning. Burn Rook’s 30 life to zero before yours runs out.

Spend ember to play cards. Each turn you gain +1 ember and draw a card. Play two cards of the same element in one turn to chain +2 power. Click a card to select it, click again to play. Keys: 1–7 select, Enter play, Space end turn.

Original game, art, names and sounds. No network, no account, no ads.
