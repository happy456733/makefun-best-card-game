**Ember Duel** — an original duel card game that plays offline in a desktop browser from its own files. You face Rook, the Cinder Knight, across a felt table: spend ember to play cards from your hand, chain same-element cards for bonus power, and burn your rival's 30 life down to zero before yours runs out. Four elements (Flame, Tide, Leaf, Stone), five card arts (Strike, Guard, Mend, Drain, Surge), a readable AI opponent that telegraphs its plans, and a full game loop: title screen, duel table, win and defeat screens.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080
```

No build step, no network requests, no dependencies — `game/` is plain static files. Keyboard: `1–7` play a card, `Enter` ends your turn, `Esc` cancels a selection. Click a card, then click a target, to play it.
